What Jomy's 10 Birthday Party
When Saturday, December 5, 11am
Where Lincoln Park Steps
California & 32nd Street
Wait, What? Kids will hike the Lands End trail to Sutro Baths

Join Jomy for a kids-only birthday hike on the Land's End Trail. We will meet at 11am at the Lincoln Park Steps. Then the kids will set off on a 1.8 mile hike to Lands End, along a beautiful hiking trail. Lizzy will walk behind the kids as a "sweeper" in case they have trouble. Abbas and Noor will meet the group at the trailhead at Sutro Baths with hot chocolate, cake and hot food. We know that a kids-only hike is not the right choice for every family. If your child is not ready to hike the trail independently, feel free to meet at Sutro Baths for the finale at 12:30. If it's raining, we'll adjust the plans. Gifts are welcome but not required or expected. Thanks for celebrating Jomy!
